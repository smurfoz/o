# Solo Marketing Ventures — Operating Context

This repo tracks two solo UK marketing service businesses. Update the
`STATUS` fields below as real clients come on — everything here should
reflect actual state, not aspiration.

## TradeReach PPC

- **What**: Google Search Ads + Local Services Ads management for
  emergency trades (boiler repair, electrical, locksmith), 1-3 van
  operations, West Midlands.
- **Offer**: £650/month standard retainer (£550/month founding rate,
  locked 6 months, first 5 clients only). Client ad spend billed
  direct by Google, typically £500-£1,500/month — never held by us.
- **Delivery**: ~4 hrs/client/month steady-state, ~10 hrs setup month.
  Call tracking via CallRail/Ringba. Weekly report: calls, cost-per-call,
  spend vs budget, one-line recommendation.
- **Capacity ceiling**: ~10 clients solo (≈40 hrs/month) before
  reporting needs a VA.
- **Site**: single-page webapp (missed-call calculator + booking) —
  published as a Claude Artifact; see chat history for the link, or
  republish from `/tmp` scratch build if the source is needed again.
- **STATUS**: Pre-launch. 0 clients signed. Founding-rate slots
  remaining: 5/5.

## Smile Local

- **What**: Local SEO + Google Business Profile management for
  independent (non-chain) dental practices, single-site, UK market
  towns.
- **Offer**: £1,200/month standard retainer (£950/month founding rate,
  locked 6 months, first 4-5 clients only).
- **Delivery**: ~8 hrs/client/month (content writing, GBP posts, review
  outreach, reporting). Content is the bottleneck, not GBP/reviews.
- **Capacity ceiling**: ~6-7 clients solo before subcontracting blog
  writing (~£40/post) to a freelancer.
- **STATUS**: Pre-launch. 0 clients signed. Founding-rate slots
  remaining: 5/5.

## Shared constraints

- **Operator**: solo, one person delivering both — total capacity
  across both ventures is capped by combined hours/month, not each
  ceiling independently. Don't plan onboarding for both at full
  capacity simultaneously without accounting for this.
- **VAT threshold**: watch combined turnover against the UK VAT
  registration threshold; flag if either venture's growth plan would
  cross it within the year.
- **No fabricated proof**: neither venture has real case studies,
  reviews, or client numbers yet. Never present placeholder/example
  figures as real results — mark them explicitly as examples until a
  real client produces real data.
