# Business Operations — TradeReach PPC & Smile Local

Ground every answer in `CLAUDE.md` at the repo root — current client
counts, founding-rate slots remaining, and capacity ceilings live there.
Update it when status changes rather than letting this skill's numbers
drift out of sync.

## Client onboarding

**TradeReach PPC** (target: setup complete within 1 week of signing):
1. Confirm Gas Safe/NICEIC registration + public liability insurance —
   needed for Local Services Ads verification, which is the slowest
   step (1-3 weeks). Submit it same day as signing, don't wait for
   the rest of setup.
2. Set up call tracking (CallRail/Ringba) with a local-area-code number.
3. Build the Search campaign (single trade per ad group, negative
   keywords from day one, geo-targeted to a realistic drive radius).
4. Confirm ad budget and billing is direct client-to-Google, never
   through us.
5. First weekly report goes out at the end of week 1 of live spend,
   even if data is thin — sets the reporting rhythm immediately.

**Smile Local** (target: first content live within 2 weeks of signing):
1. Audit + fix Google Business Profile (categories, photos, booking
   link) in week 1.
2. Set up the review-generation sequence (post-appointment text/email).
3. First 2 blog posts written and published by end of week 2, targeting
   the practice's actual private-treatment services — never generic
   dental content.
4. First monthly report scheduled for day 30, covering ranking movement
   and review count change.

Both: get the founding-rate case-study agreement in writing before
setup starts, if the client is on that rate — don't assume it's implied.

## Weekly / monthly reporting

- **TradeReach PPC (weekly)**: calls (answered vs missed), cost per
  call, spend vs budget, any LSA lead disputes filed/credited, one-line
  recommendation. One page, no jargon.
- **Smile Local (monthly)**: keyword ranking movement, review count
  change, GBP views/actions, content published. One page, no jargon.
- Never report a number as a result unless it's real client data —
  founding-rate examples and calculator estimates stay clearly labeled
  as examples, in client-facing material as much as in ours.

## Pricing & capacity rules

- **Founding-rate sunset**: once a venture's founding-rate slots (see
  `CLAUDE.md`) are full, new signups go on the standard rate
  immediately — don't extend founding pricing informally to keep
  closing deals. Decrement the slots-remaining count in `CLAUDE.md`
  the same day a founding-rate client signs.
- **Raise-price trigger**: once 2-3 real case studies exist for a
  venture (real ranking/call-volume movement, not calculator
  estimates), move new-signup pricing up (TradeReach: £650 → £750;
  Smile Local: £1,200 → £1,400) — existing clients keep their signed
  rate until their renewal point, never mid-contract.
- **Capacity ceiling is a hard stop, not a target**: don't sign a
  client that would push either venture past the hours ceiling in
  `CLAUDE.md` without first lining up a subcontractor (Smile Local:
  freelance content writer) or a VA (TradeReach: weekly reporting).
- **Combined-capacity check**: before signing a new client on either
  venture, check total committed hours/month across *both* ventures
  against realistic solo monthly hours (~150-160 hrs before quality
  degrades) — a healthy ceiling on one venture alone can still overload
  the operator if the other is also near capacity.

## Renewals & churn

- Flag any client approaching their 3-month (TradeReach) or trial
  (Smile Local) minimum 2 weeks out — renewal conversations happen
  before the deadline, not after.
- A client cancelling: capture the real reason (budget cut, dissatisfaction,
  business closed) before writing it off as generic churn — thin-margin
  or beachhead-exhaustion risk should update the venture's risk notes,
  not just the client count.
- Don't backfill a cancelled slot with a founding rate once the
  founding period (first N clients) has closed, even if capacity frees
  up — the standard rate applies to every signup after the original
  founding batch, cancellations included.

## What this skill is not for

Inventing new business ideas, ranking concepts, or exploring a third
venture — use `business-marketing-ideas` for that. This skill assumes
TradeReach PPC and Smile Local already exist and is about running them
well, not deciding whether to start them.
