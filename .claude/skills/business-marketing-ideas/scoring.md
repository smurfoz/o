# Scoring Rubric

Score every idea on the same six criteria, 0-5 each (30 total). Score what's
actually stated in the idea block — an idea with vague unit economics or a
vague beachhead scores low on those criteria even if a strong version is
plausible in theory.

| Criterion | 0-1 | 2-3 | 4-5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo deliverability** — can one person actually run this | Needs a team or specialist skills the operator can't credibly cover alone | Doable solo but delivery will strain past a handful of clients | Cleanly deliverable by one person at the stated client count, with a clear point where subcontracting could extend it |
| **Beachhead specificity** — is the first market real and findable | "UK small businesses" or equivalent — no way to identify or reach buyers | A named vertical or segment, but no clear channel to find them | Named vertical + size band + a specific, nameable place to find them (a directory, network, marketplace, existing relationship) |
| **Unit economics** — does the money work at solo scale | No real numbers, or margin implausible once real hours are counted | Plausible margin but thin, or depends on volume a solo operator can't sustain | Clear per-client margin at realistic UK rates and hours that a solo operator could live on |
| **90-day credibility** — will the first clients actually appear | No concrete answer for where client #1 comes from | A plausible channel, but timeline or conversion is optimistic | Specific channel/relationship with a realistic timeline to 1-3 paying clients |
| **Differentiation** — why a buyer picks this over the obvious alternative | Identical to widely-available agency offers, no edge | A meaningful angle (niche focus, packaging, price point) | Hard for a generalist agency or freelancer to match quickly — real expertise, access, or positioning edge |
| **Durability** — survives past the first few clients | Beachhead exhausts after a handful of clients, or churn likely kills it fast | Some runway but will need a real expansion move soon | Beachhead + expansion path together support ongoing revenue past the initial push |

## How to use it

- **Score against the block, not the idea's best-case version.** Vague unit
  economics or a generic beachhead should score low even when a sharper
  version of the same idea might score well — that's the incentive to
  sharpen the idea, not inflate the score.
- **Solo deliverability is a gate, not just a criterion.** An idea scoring
  0-1 here (genuinely needs a team) should be flagged as disqualified rather
  than just ranked low — it doesn't meet the brief.
- **Weight by the user's stated constraint.** If they said "I need income
  fast," weight 90-day credibility and unit economics higher when picking
  the top recommendation, even over a higher raw total.
- **Ties:** prefer the idea with the higher Beachhead specificity + 90-day
  credibility combined score — a idea with a real, near-term path to first
  clients beats a theoretically bigger one with a vaguer start.
- **Score bands:**
  - 24-30: strong candidate, worth prioritizing
  - 15-23: viable but has a real gap — name it explicitly
  - 0-14: keep for contrast only, don't recommend without flagging why it
    scored low
