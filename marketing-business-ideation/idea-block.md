# Idea Block Template

Use this exact structure for every idea so ideas are easy to scan and compare
side by side. Keep each field tight — this is a pitch, not a business plan.

```
### [Idea Name — short, memorable, not generic]

**The idea:** 2-3 sentences. What it is and how it works, concretely.
Someone unfamiliar with the project should understand the mechanism from
this alone.

**Target audience:** Who this is for, specifically. "Small business owners"
is too broad; "solo e-commerce sellers doing $5k-50k/month who run their own
Instagram" is usable.

**Problem / opportunity:** The gap this exploits or the pain it solves. Why
does this need to exist right now?

**Mechanism / channel:** How it actually reaches people or generates
revenue — the specific channel, format, or growth loop. Name the platform,
partnership type, or pricing mechanic, not just "marketing."

**Why this, why now:** The differentiator or timing edge — an existing
trend, an underserved niche, a capability that wasn't cheap/possible before,
a gap competitors are leaving open.

**Effort to launch:** S / M / L — rough read on time and resources to get a
first version live (a landing page + waitlist is S; a two-sided marketplace
is L).

**Upside if it works:** What growth or revenue looks like at a meaningful
(not maximal) scale — order of magnitude, not a fabricated forecast.

**Biggest risk:** The one thing most likely to sink it (no distribution
channel, thin margins, regulatory friction, requires behavior change from
users, etc.).
```

## Guidance

- Every field should be fillable in one to two sentences. If a field needs a
  paragraph, the idea is underspecified — sharpen it before scoring it.
- "Biggest risk" is not optional filler — an idea block with no real risk
  named usually means the idea hasn't been thought through, not that it's
  risk-free.
- Reuse concrete details from the user's own request (their product, their
  audience, their existing channels) rather than defaulting to generic
  placeholders — a generic idea block is a sign the idea itself is generic.
