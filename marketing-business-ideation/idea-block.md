# Idea Block Template

Use this exact structure for every solo UK marketing business idea. All eight
fields are required — an idea missing unit economics, a beachhead, or a
90-day path is not ready to score.

```
### [Business Name — short, memorable, not generic]

**The idea:** 2-3 sentences. The specific marketing service, who delivers it
(one person), and how it's packaged. Should be understandable without
jargon.

**Offer:** What's actually sold, in sellable-package form — the service
scope, format (retainer / project / one-off), and price point. "SEO
services" is not an offer; "a £1,200/month local-SEO retainer covering
Google Business Profile, 4 blog posts, and monthly reporting for a single
location" is.

**Beachhead:** The specific first slice of the UK market to target — a
vertical, a company size band, and a region or channel where they cluster.
One sentence, specific enough to name where to find these buyers (a trade
body, a local business network, a marketplace, an industry Slack/Facebook
group).

**Unit economics:** What it costs the operator in time to deliver one
client per month, what it's sold for, and what's left over. State the
assumptions (hourly-equivalent rate, hours/client/month, any hard costs —
ad spend passthrough, tools, subcontracted work). Show it as a rough
per-client margin, not a company-wide forecast.

**Beachhead-to-scale path:** Once the beachhead is proven, the natural next
segment or expansion lever (a second vertical, a second region, productising
into a template, bringing on a subcontractor for delivery) — one sentence,
not a five-year plan.

**90-day path:** Weeks 1-4 (how the first 1-3 clients are actually found and
closed — name the concrete channel or relationship), weeks 5-8 (delivery +
what's being learned/adjusted), weeks 9-12 (target client count/revenue and
what decides whether to keep going). Concrete actions, not "build
awareness."

**Why this, why now:** The gap or timing edge in the UK market specifically
— a regulatory change, a channel getting cheaper/more crowded elsewhere, an
underserved vertical, a skill the operator already has that's rare in this
niche.

**Biggest risk:** The one thing most likely to sink it — thin margins at
solo scale, a beachhead too small to sustain, delivery bottlenecking on one
person, client churn, a channel getting harder to sell into.
```

## Guidance

- If "Unit economics" doesn't produce a number the operator could actually
  live on (or a clear path to one), the idea isn't done — sharpen the offer
  or beachhead until it does.
- "Beachhead" that reduces to "small businesses in the UK" is a sign the idea
  itself is unfocused — push for the specific vertical and where they
  cluster.
- The 90-day path should survive the question "where does client #1
  literally come from" with a real answer, not a channel-shaped hope.
